Finance

Control your entire financial flow with invoices, purchase/sales orders, POS sales, quotes, advances and automated receipts (validating legal date ranges), while managing tax data (BN, GST/HST) and mass communications via SMS or other channels for marketing, collections, orders or alerts. Additionally, integrate decentralized finance: execute Bitcoin transactions (signatures, staking, collateralized loans using BTC with Taproot smart contracts), perform express exchange (Lightning/on-chain to fiat in linked accounts) and operate with full regulatory compliance. All on a flexible platform where each area operates autonomously but synchronized, with no irregular practices, even in DeFi environments.

Inventory

Our inventory module operates in a decentralized manner, allowing you to manage stock, adjust prices and update descriptions or meta tags from any location or device. Each point of sale, warehouse or work team can update information in real time without depending on a central core, including autonomous management of your online store, publication of campaigns on multiple platforms and localized management of suppliers or customers. Data is dynamically synchronized between all nodes, maintaining traceability of materials, equipment and finished products while adapting to the specific needs of each operation.

Logistics

At Cash on Delivery (C) we've redefined logistics with a unique model where our Shipping Insurance acts as a neutral guarantor (2 of 3) in each transaction, standardizing and ensuring exact compliance between all parties. We've automated the entire cycle: intelligent waybill generation, integration with partner carriers (Canada Post, Purolator, etc.) and live tracking with automatic notifications. But our true differentiator is the cash on delivery system with 100% certified effectiveness, where our insurance company intervenes 24/7 as a technical mediator - validating conditions, resolving PQR in real time and applying proactive security protocols for high-value goods. More than a module, it's a secure ecosystem where each shipment operates with the precision of a smart contract: executable, auditable and backed by our compliance guarantee.
